Liar's Table — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026.

Liar's Table is a single-player puzzle game. Your level progress, chips, star ratings, and settings are saved only on your own device, using local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this information on a server, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any account.

Advertising

This version of the game has no ads. If a future version adds ads through Google AdMob, this policy will be updated first, and AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads. You will be able to manage ad personalization through your device's settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device is registered with Firebase Cloud Messaging (a Google service) so we can send occasional announcements, such as new content or updates. This uses a device identifier managed by Firebase; we do not collect your name or other personal details through this feature.

Children

Liar's Table is not directed at children and is not designed to collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the current version of this policy.
